How to keep a houseplant alive through winter
Most houseplants that die in winter are loved to death. Light falls, growth slows, and a plant that drank every few days in July now sits in wet soil for a week. Water less, move pots closer to the window, and hold the fertiliser until spring.
Watch the leaves rather than the calendar: drooping that recovers within a day of watering means thirsty, while yellowing lower leaves usually mean too much. A cheap moisture meter takes out the guesswork.
